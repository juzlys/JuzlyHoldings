The Structural Problem

Why Month End Reporting Takes Three Weeks and Still Gets Disputed

The delay is not a people problem. It is a structural one: your finance, operations, and sales data live in separate systems with no single point of synchronisation. Every report is assembled manually, and every manual assembly introduces a new opportunity for discrepancy.

Data Architecture

Disconnected Source Systems

Odoo, Xero, Salesforce, and custom operational databases each hold fragments of the truth. Juzly connects them all under a single governed data pipeline, with no manual export or copy and paste steps in the chain.

Latency Reduction

The 10 to 21 Day Decision Lag

Decisions made on last month's numbers are decisions made with incomplete situational awareness. Our automated pipelines mean your dashboards reflect this morning's transactions, not last fortnight's batch exports.

Real-Time Alerts

Retrospective Anomaly Detection

By the time a spreadsheet flags a variance, the cause is three weeks old. Our live anomaly alert layer surfaces deviations at the moment they occur, when corrective action still has commercial value.

Intelligence Architecture

Three Layers That Replace Your Reporting Cycle

Juzly does not resell a SaaS dashboard tool. Every pipeline and dashboard is built to your specific data schema, owned by your organisation, and free from any third party vendor lock in.

Pipeline Streaming

Automated Data Pipeline

Raw transactional data from Odoo, Xero, Salesforce, and your custom APIs flows continuously into a governed cloud data warehouse. No scheduled batch jobs. No manual triggers. No overnight wait.

Executive Cockpits

Role Based Executive Dashboards

Your CFO, COO, and sales director each see the metrics relevant to their mandate, drawn from the same verified data source. No more version conflicts between the finance deck and the operations report.

Algorithmic Guards

Live Anomaly Alerts

Threshold based alerts notify the relevant team member the moment a KPI breaches its operating boundary, from inventory below reorder level to a department spend rate exceeding its monthly forecast.
