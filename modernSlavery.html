Modern Slavery & Ethical Sourcing Statement

Juzly Integrated Solutions Ltd. is unequivocally committed to operating ethically, transparently, and with zero tolerance for any form of modern slavery, human trafficking, forced labor, or worker exploitation across our direct operations and global partner networks.

Compliance Framework: Modern Slavery Act 2018 (Cth) | Financial Year 2026–2027
Modern Slavery Act 2018 (Cth) · Zero-Tolerance Governance
Ethical Sourcing · Statutory Compliance
Corporate Structure

Our Operations & Dual-Hub Model

Clear governance lines ensure labor compliance across both of our primary operational centers and distributed squads.

Headquarters Oversight

Australia Strategic Headquarters

Our Australian executive leadership directly oversees enterprise contracts, board governance, client advisory services, and statutory compliance under Commonwealth modern slavery legislation.

Engineering Directorate

Sri Lanka Operational Center

Our dedicated Colombo technology delivery center employs technical engineers, Odoo developers, and CRM architects under fair, above-market compensation packages with clear statutory labor protections.

Fair Work Standards

Direct Employment Guarantee

100% of our core staff hold formal, written employment agreements. We prohibit recruitment fees, passport retention, and involuntary overtime across all business units.

Risk Assessment

Supply Chain Due Diligence & Vendor Standards

We systematically evaluate our supplier tiers to identify and eliminate modern slavery risks before engagement.

Cloud Infrastructure

Cloud & Software Infrastructure

We partner exclusively with enterprise tier-1 infrastructure providers (Google Cloud, AWS, Odoo, HubSpot) maintaining certified ISO 27001, SOC 2, and rigorous global human rights compliance policies.

Logistics Audits

Event Equipment & Logistics

For our Corporate Event Management division, all physical staging contractors, audiovisual vendors, and on-site logistics crew undergo mandatory fair-work and safety pre-qualification audits.

Contractual Safeguards

Mandatory Contract Clauses

All supplier and vendor contracts include strict representations guaranteeing adherence to anti-slavery laws, with explicit rights of termination for non-compliance or labor violations.

Grievance Protocols

Whistleblowing & Remediation Framework

Ensuring immediate, secure, and protected avenues for reporting ethical concerns or suspected labor violations.

Grievance Channel

Confidential Reporting Channel

Employees, contractors, and third-party workers may confidentially or anonymously report suspected human rights or labor violations directly to our governance team at compliance@juzly.net.

All reports are acknowledged within 48 hours and investigated independently by executive directors.

Non-Retaliation Policy

Zero Retaliation & Corrective Action

Juzly guarantees complete protection from adverse employment action or commercial retaliation for any whistleblower raising concerns in good faith. If an issue is identified, our priority is safe remediation and support for affected individuals.

Continued failure by any supplier to remediate labor discrepancies results in permanent disqualification and contract termination.

Board Approval

Executive Accountability & Continuous Improvement

This statement has been prepared in accordance with statutory expectations and approved by the leadership of Juzly Integrated Solutions Ltd. We conduct annual audits of our procurement practices, review partner credentials, and provide ongoing guidance to our delivery squad leads.
