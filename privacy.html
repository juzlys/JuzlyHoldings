Data Governance & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Juzly Integrated Solutions Ltd. adheres to the highest standards of data integrity, confidentiality, and statutory privacy protection across all our enterprise advisory and digital engineering engagements.

Regulatory Alignment: Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) & Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) | Effective 2026
Information Scope

Information We Collect & Hold

We only collect personal and commercial information reasonably necessary to formulate, deploy, and govern enterprise client solutions.

Direct Business Inquiries

Name, corporate email address, telephone numbers, employer organization, job title, and project specifications submitted via scoping forms or RFP inquiries.

Technical & Engagement Data

IP addresses, browser telemetry, referrers, and session analytics gathered via strictly essential cookies to optimize site performance and security authentication.

Enterprise Solution Telemetry

ERP configuration records, ETL system logs, and event registration details managed strictly under executed Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) and Client Master Service Agreements (MSAs).

Cross-Border Transfer

Cross-Border Data Flows & Security Controls

Rigorous operational safeguards govern information transfer between our Australian advisory headquarters and Colombo delivery center.

Dual-Hub Encryption Protocols

All cross-hub communications, codebase repositories, and client data transit through TLS 1.3 encrypted pipelines. Data at rest is secured via AES-256 encryption within sovereign, ISO 27001-certified cloud regions.

  • Zero unencrypted plain-text transmissions
  • Role-based access control (RBAC) with MFA enforcement

Compliance with APP 8 (Cross-Border Disclosures)

Prior to transferring any personal information overseas, Juzly takes reasonable steps to ensure that the recipient entity upholds Australian Privacy Principles equivalent protections, enforced through binding corporate intra-group data agreements.

  • Binding intra-group data governance agreements
  • Regular data compliance audits across Colombo facilities
Your Rights

Access, Correction & Privacy Inquiries

You have the right under Australian privacy law to inspect, amend, or request deletion of personal information held by our firm.

Exercising Privacy Rights

You may request access to any personal information we hold about you or request corrections if you believe our records are inaccurate, incomplete, or out of date. We respond to all verified statutory requests within 30 calendar days.
