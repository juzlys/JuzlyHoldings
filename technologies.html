ERP Delivery

Full Lifecycle Odoo ERP Implementation in Three Verified Phases

Most ERP projects fail during configuration because the vendor begins building before understanding the operational reality. Juzly uses a phased gate structure where each phase is demonstrated and approved by your leadership team before the next phase begins. You approve working, testable deliverables, not slide decks.

Discovery Phase

Phase 1: Architecture and Gap Analysis

A full operational audit maps your current process flows, data sources, and integration requirements against a purpose built Odoo module roadmap. No assumptions are made about your existing infrastructure.

Module Engineering

Phase 2: Custom Engineering and Configuration

Purpose built Python module development, third party API connectors for logistics, finance, HR, and ecommerce layers, multi currency and multi entity configuration, and UI customisation aligned to your operating structure.

Deployment & QA

Phase 3: Migration and Hypercare

Automated data migration from legacy systems eliminates manual reentry risk. User role training, user acceptance testing, and a structured hypercare support period follow each deployment.

Supply Chain Governance

Multi Warehouse Logistics and Strategic Procurement Built into Your ERP

Supply chain discipline is embedded into the platform itself, not managed through a separate spreadsheet layer alongside your ERP. Inventory replenishment, vendor evaluation, and procurement compliance operate automatically from within Odoo.

Logistics Infrastructure

Multi Warehouse and Third Party Logistics

Advanced barcode scanning, dropshipping routes, cross docking rules, and real time inventory synchronisation across distributed distribution hubs, all visible from a single Odoo operations dashboard.

  • Automated reordering triggers and demand forecasting models aligned to your lead times
  • Full batch, lot, and serial number traceability from supplier receipt to customer delivery
Procurement Controls

Strategic Procurement and Vendor Governance

Purchase requisitions route through multi tier approval workflows based on your budget thresholds. Every vendor is scored on delivery performance, ESG compliance, and certificate validity, with procurement blocked automatically for non compliant suppliers.

  • Three way invoice matching between purchase orders, goods receipts, and supplier invoices
  • Automated supplier certificate expiry tracking with threshold based procurement blocking
