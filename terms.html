Commercial Scope

Enterprise Engagement & Delivery Framework

Our engagements execute under bilateral Master Service Agreements (MSAs) and Statement of Work (SOW) attachments.

Statements of Work (SOWs)

Each technical engagement (Odoo ERP deployment, BI ETL pipeline, RevOps campaign, or event execution) is bounded by a formal SOW detailing milestones, acceptance criteria, and fee structures.

Service Level Agreements (SLAs)

We provide 99.9% uptime commitments for managed hosting environments, guaranteed 4-hour critical issue response windows, and dedicated sprint team velocity tracking.

Dual-Hub Quality Governance

All software deliverables engineered by our Colombo delivery squads undergo rigorous QA gates reviewed and signed off by Australian-certified solution architects.

IP & Protection

Intellectual Property & Mutual Confidentiality

Uncompromising protection of client trade secrets, proprietary algorithms, and enterprise assets.

Client Ownership of Deliverables

Upon full settlement of milestone invoices, all bespoke code modules, configured database schemas, ETL pipelines, and marketing assets developed specifically for the client transfer to client ownership.

  • Full assignment of bespoke custom code
  • Open source component license compliance

Perpetual Confidentiality

Both parties agree to protect and keep strictly confidential all commercial strategies, financial data, and technical specifications disclosed during scoping and delivery, remaining in effect perpetually.

  • Standard NDA execution before project onboarding
  • Strict non-solicitation covenants for squad engineers
Legal Governance

Liability Standards, Governing Law & Jurisdiction

Clear statutory limits and dispute resolution pathways protecting both parties.

Limitation of Liability

Except for breaches of confidentiality or gross negligence, each party's aggregate liability under any engagement shall not exceed the total fees paid by the client under the applicable Statement of Work in the preceding 12 months.

These terms and all related agreements shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of Victoria, Australia. The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of Victorian courts.
